ROUND 17 · EXAMPLES, NOT THE BUILD

The app in three verbs

The app still wears the planner (Your Places · Today · Pulse · You). These are three ways the home screen and tab bar could read as KEEP · GIVE · PASS ON instead — daylight world, planner behind a door. Same demo user (Maya), same data.

A · The shelf

Home is your shelf of collections through time — each with its own GIFT door and its echo counts. Received gifts sit below in their givers’ gold lanes. Two tabs + a trip door.

OPEN A ⌲ B · The three verbs

The tab bar IS the loop: KEEP (save, import, your collection) · GIVE (the composer, your links, the echoes) · PASS ON (what you received, in lanes, ready to take from). Nothing else at the top level.

OPEN B ⌲ C · The passport

Home is you, public-first: your avatar, your one link (prevoya.app/g/you), your collections as a grid — the Instagram-highlights mental model. Give is one tap from the top.

OPEN C ⌲ D · The expert cut

The verdict of two blind product reviews (26 Nov): skeleton A, C’s covers and link pill, B’s link echoes and story card one tap deep — plus the two things all three missed: a living “This week” lane and the ask row.
