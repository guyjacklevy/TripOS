# The Pivot — where Prevoya is going

*Last updated: 21 Nov 2026. This document is the map. If something ships and
this file doesn't reflect it, the ship isn't done.*

## What Prevoya is now

**The fastest way to keep the places you discover — and give them to the
people who ask.**

The old product was an AI trip planner. The pivot (8 Nov) makes the
collection the product and the gift the growth engine. AI planning still
exists, but as a tool inside the product, not the front door.

## The loop, in three verbs

Everything the user does maps to one of three verbs. Any surface that
doesn't serve one of them is planner heritage and must earn its place or
move behind a door.

1. **KEEP** — save places you find, stamp places you've been, let your
   trip photos become your collection (the photo import).
2. **GIVE** — when someone asks "where should I go?", send your collection
   as one link. The gift page plays a ceremony and makes your taste look
   as good as it is. The echo tells you it landed (opened · played · took).
3. **PASS ON** — the receiver keeps what they love, lives their own trip,
   and the relay turns them into the next giver (three one-time moments:
   "Yours begins", the lineage line, "Three keepers — that's a gift").

## Open strategic questions (Guy's calls, not dev's)

- **How much planner do we show?** A collection-only user now never sees the
  planner uninvited (Round 12.5). The deeper question: does a trip owner's
  app lead with the collection too, with Today/route as trip-time tools?
- **When does global unlock?** Ruling stands: when the first real gift
  chains move. The code is ready to derive copy from `trips.destination`.
- **What is the organic front door worth?** The pivot bets on receiving a
  gift as the main way in. The public shelf stays as substrate, not engine.
