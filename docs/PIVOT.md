# The Pivot — where Prevoya is going

*Last updated: 24 Nov 2026. This document is the map. If something ships and
this file doesn't reflect it, the ship isn't done.*

## Where the pivot stands (Guy's ruling, 24 Nov 2026)

**The machinery is done; the face is not.** Rounds 6–14c are all shipped and
phone-proven (see CHANGELOG.md): save anywhere on earth, collections like
highlights with a link each, the gift ceremony, the receiver's clean landing,
the relay, import-first (camera roll → collection in ~3 minutes), and the
automated Monday K readout — whose first run (23 Nov) measured the funnel
live and found the first chain (K = 1.0 on a sample of one).

But the product a stranger meets has **not fully pivoted**:

- **The app still wears the planner.** Tabs are YOUR PLACES / TODAY / PULSE
  / YOU. Inside You live the passport, packing + repack mode, flight prep.
  Today and Pulse are trip-planner surfaces. None of these serve
  KEEP / GIVE / PASS ON, and they sit at the same level as the collection.
- **The landing is planner-era.** "Never lose a place you discover" over a
  flight-board metaphor; destination tiles saying "In research" (Chiang Mai,
  Tulum, Lisboa) — wrong claim for a global product; "take the full tour"
  leads to the Bali curated shelf whose purpose a stranger can't name; the
  closing section is a boarding ticket — the old design language.
- **"For slow max" class of copy** — positioning lines that no longer match
  what the product is.

**Next phase (ordered by Guy, 24 Nov):**
1. Log and secure everything built so far (this update + CHANGELOG + git).
2. **The anonymous visitor** — a fresh pair of eyes with zero context walks
   prevoya.app and reports what they understood the product to be, section
   by section. Findings go on the record before any design decision.
3. **Then design everything** from those findings: as simple as possible,
   easy to understand, beautiful — a feeling of vacation, real photos,
   realistic. Every surface either serves KEEP / GIVE / PASS ON or moves
   behind a door.

No design work starts before the audit is read. First sends wait until the

## What Prevoya is now

**The fastest way to keep the places you discover — and give them to the
people who ask.**

The old product was an AI trip planner. The pivot (8 Nov) makes the
collection the product and the gift the growth engine. AI planning still
exists, but as a tool inside the product, not the front door.

## The loop, in three verbs

Everything the user does maps to one of three verbs. Any surface that
doesn't serve one of them is planner heritage and must earn its place or
move behind a door.

1. **KEEP** — save places you find, stamp places you've been, let your
   trip photos become your collection (the photo import).
2. **GIVE** — when someone asks "where should I go?", send your collection
   as one link. The gift page plays a ceremony and makes your taste look
   as good as it is. The echo tells you it landed (opened · played · took).
3. **PASS ON** — the receiver keeps what they love, lives their own trip,
   and the relay turns them into the next giver (three one-time moments:
   "Yours begins", the lineage line, "Three keepers — that's a gift").

**The roles are not exclusive (Guy, 21 Nov).** Every user is both sender
and receiver. You can receive from several people at once — each gift
lives in its own giver's lane, so you always see *who was where* — and you
choose, place by place, what crosses from any received collection into
your own. Your own collection is what you give; theirs stay theirs until
you take from them.

## Rulings (Guy, 21 Nov 2026)

- **The planner is a tool, not the face.** The collection leads for
  everyone; the flow must be simple enough to need no teaching
  (KEEP / GIVE / PASS ON). Planner surfaces serve the trip, behind doors.
- **GLOBAL FROM THE FIRST MOMENT.** Don't think Bali — think anyone on any
  trip, especially travelers and influencers who want their places kept
  like Instagram highlights, but *their own collections*: wherever they
  go, they save, and each collection has its own link to send. This
  supersedes the earlier "parked until chains move" ruling. In build
  terms: the *collection layer* (save, stamp, import, gift) works
  anywhere on earth; the *curated layer* (the vetted shelf with prices
  and areas) grows destination by destination, Bali first — content, not
  architecture.
- **Organic stays substrate.** The gift is the engine; the public shelf
  gives receivers and savers depth, it doesn't carry growth.

## Measurement

Two layers, both in the Monday readout ([K-SYSTEM.md](K-SYSTEM.md)):

- **Activity** — is the product being used: saves, stamps, sends.
- **K** — does one user bring the next: the funnel from send to chain.
