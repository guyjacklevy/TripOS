# The Flow — first value and time-to-value, per actor

*Last updated: 21 Nov 2026. Three actors move through Prevoya: the
receiver, the sender, and the organic newcomer. For each: the flow step by
step, the first value they get, how long it takes, and the levers that
shorten it.*

## 1. The receiver (our strongest surface)

**Flow today:**
1. A friend's WhatsApp message: one personal line + one link.
2. Tap → the ceremony plays. No login, no install, no app store. Real
   places, real photos, the friend's marks (been / worth it / saved).
3. "Take all" — one tap, still no account.
4. "Start your own collection" → email code → lands on Your Bali with the
   gift in its giver's gold lane, open, explained.
5. ⚑ any place to make it theirs ("Yours begins") … → the relay carries
   them toward their own first gift.

**First value:** the answer to "where should I go?" from someone they
trust, beautiful, in **~10 seconds, zero commitment**. This is the best
time-to-value in the product and the reason the gift is the front door.

**Friction left:** the signup (email + code) sits between "I took it" and
"it's safe". The taken list already survives in the browser, but the
promise "sign up = don't lose it" is the only reason to log in — keep it
that honest.

## 2. The sender

**Flow today:**
1. Precondition: a collection worth giving — saves, stamps, photos.
2. The gift door on Your Bali ("Your Bali · 51 places · ⤴ GIFT IT").
3. Composer: pick keepers vs everything, a personal note, photos consent.
4. Copy message + link → paste in WhatsApp, person to person.
5. The echo comes back as counts: OPENED · PLAYED · TOOK.

**First value (before any send):** their own trip, organized — places,
stamps, their photos. **First value of sending:** the ceremony makes their
taste look as good as it is, and the echo is recognition without exposure
(counts, never names).

**The time-to-value problem:** a collection normally takes a trip to build
— days to weeks. **The photo import collapses this to ~2 minutes**: the
camera roll already recorded the trip (GPS + time in the photos); the
import turns ~60 photos into a stamped, photo-backed collection in one
pass. This is the sender's TTV weapon.

**The biggest open lever:** the import is currently a power-user door
(appears after 10 stamps). For the pivot it should be an *onboarding*
door: "just back from a trip? your camera roll is already a collection."
A returned traveler should reach a giftable collection in their first
session. — *Proposed, not yet built; Guy's call.*

## 3. The organic newcomer

**Flow today:** lands on prevoya.app or the public shelf → browses curated
Bali → saves (buffered without an account) → signs up → collection-first
boot (no forced planner since Round 12.5) → the planner is a door on Today.

**First value:** a credible, curated destination list with real prices and
areas. Honest, but generic — Google Maps and TikTok do discovery too. The
pivot's answer: **organic is substrate, not engine.** The engine is the
gift; the shelf exists so receivers and savers have depth to explore.

## The capture question (the Maps share shortcut)

Guy's skepticism is correct, and the honest technical picture:

- **Android:** share-to-Prevoya works after installing the PWA. Real, but
  install-gated.
- **iOS:** the web platform does not support share targets for PWAs at
  all; our iOS path is a hand-installed Shortcut. Mainstream users will
  never do this. It stays a power-user trick, not a strategy.
- **What works for everyone, today:** the paste box (`/app/save`) — paste
  a Google Maps link or type a name, zero install. This should be the
  advertised capture mechanic until there's a native app (a native share
  extension is the real "faceless" capture, and it waits until the loop
  proves itself).

**The key strategic point: the loop does not depend on wild capture.** It
runs on *lived trips* — stamps while there, photo import after. Capture
from other apps is an amplifier, not the engine.

## Time-to-value levers, ranked (dev view)

1. **Import-first onboarding** — sender TTV from "a trip's worth of
   effort" to ~2 minutes. The single biggest lever on the board.
2. **Gift useful before signup** — the receiver's taken list should work
   on the ground (areas, map) before any account; signup stays the honest
   "keep it safe" moment.
3. **The three-verb simplicity pass** — the app's surfaces regrouped so a
   first-time user reads KEEP / GIVE / PASS ON without being taught;
   planner surfaces live behind the trip, not in front of the collection.
4. **The brand sweep** — the words match the story everywhere ("Concierge"
   off the landing board, planner-voice strings out of the app).
